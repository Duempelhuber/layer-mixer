Layer Mixer – Change Log
========================

v0.06 (2026-10-25)
------------------
Added
- Warhammer Dry range: 29 paints in 6 colour groups, selectable for
  recipes; Dry metallics (2) for reference only

Changed
- Reduced on-screen text: header hint, chart legend and info boxes removed;
  footer notes shortened to one line
- Swatch tooltip shows the layer a click will fill
- Paint types shown as "Base paint" / "Layer paint"
- Reference-only paints are dimmed
- "Paints needed" appears once the recipe contains a paint
- Examples and saved recipes combined in one "Recipes" section
- A single paint is shaded on the sphere
- Short hint below the sphere: drag to move the light
- Warhammer Shade grouped by colour
- Metallic and shade markers on swatches are now visible; metallics brighter
- Warhammer Colour swatches marked B (Base) or L (Layer) instead of a corner notch
- Warpfiend Grey listed as Layer paint

Removed
- Warhammer Colour: Ceramite White (discontinued)


v0.05 (2026-10-25)
------------------
Removed
- Edge highlight layer; recipes now have five layers


v0.04 (2026-10-24)
------------------
Changed
- Position numbers are shown only for paint series (Tone Pro,
  Warpaints Fanatic); removed from Warhammer Shade


v0.03 (2026-10-24)
------------------
Changed
- Footer credit now reads "Vibecoded by Duempelhuber"
- Revised change log


v0.02 (2026-10-24)
------------------
Added
- Warhammer Colour range: 130 Base and Layer paints in 18 colour groups
- Base paint indicator on swatches
- Warhammer Colour metallics and Warhammer Shade (reference only, not
  selectable for recipes)
- Brand tabs with range selection
- Recipe counters per brand and range

Changed
- Long colour groups wrap to a second line
- Each brand restores the last selected range


v0.01 (2026-10-24)
------------------
Initial release.

Added
- Warhammer Tone Pro: 30 colours, 5 tones each
- The Army Painter Warpaints Fanatic: 27 colour ranges, 170 paints
- Six-layer recipe: Deep shadow, Shadow, Base, Highlight, Top highlight,
  Edge highlight
- Combination of paints from different ranges in one recipe
- Sphere preview with smooth and stepped blending
- Adjustable light direction (mouse drag, arrow keys)
- Paints needed list
- Copy recipe to clipboard
- Example recipes; saving of custom recipes
- Offline operation as a single HTML file

Fixed
- Warpaints Fanatic, Black & Greys: position of Ironclad Grey
