Layer Mixer – Change Log
========================

v0.12 (2026-10-27)
------------------
Added
- Start screen with an overview of your paints per brand and range and
  a short guide; a range in the address (e.g. #tp) opens it directly
- Clear in My paints removes all paints and saved recipes after a
  confirmation

Changed
- Brands and ranges are listed in a sidebar on the left, so more brands
  fit without crowding the top of the page
- One brand is open at a time; a pinned brand stays open
- The All / Mine filter is always shown in My paints and stays inactive
  until a paint is marked
- The chart uses two columns whenever the window is wide enough
- Clearer note that screen colours are a planning aid


v0.11 (2026-10-26)
------------------
Added
- My paints: mark the paints you own by click, drag or row name; counts
  per brand and range
- Mine filter shows only your paints; Paints needed highlights the
  paints you are missing
- Save and Load your paints and recipes as a file; in Chrome and Edge,
  Save as chooses the file and Save updates it
- Help window with a short guide and an overview of all marks

Changed
- Black, grey and white are separate colour groups; a group is only
  shown when the range has paints in it


v0.10 (2026-10-26)
------------------
Added
- The Army Painter: Warpaints Air, Colour Primer, Speedpaint, Wash and
  Effects; Warpaints Fanatic Masterclass paints and metallics
- Monument Hobbies: Pro Acryl, Expert Acrylics, Prime, 1-Step, Wash and
  Specials

Changed
- Colour groups split into single colours in every range
- Products without a colour of their own are marked M (medium),
  V (varnish) or P (primer)
- Range names follow the manufacturers; Warhammer Colour is now
  Base & Layer
- Tone Pro rows show only the colour name

Fixed
- Seven swatches of a Warpaints Fanatic range fit on one line again


v0.09 (2026-10-26)
------------------
Added
- Warhammer Air: 60 paints; metallics and clear paints for reference
- Warhammer Primer: spray primers and Imperial Primer; metallic primers
  for reference
- Warhammer Contrast (61 paints) and Technical (25 paints), for reference

Changed
- New look: dark grey surfaces, new typeface, selection marked with
  a brush stroke
- Reference-only ranges are marked with an eye and listed after the
  usable ranges
- Contrast swatches show how the paint collects in recesses

Removed
- Warhammer Shade: the four gloss shades (discontinued)


v0.08 (2026-10-25)
------------------
Added
- Preview forms: sphere, cylinder, cone, block and cloth
- Light strength setting for the size of the highlights

Changed
- Each layer covers a fixed share of the form: base largest, top highlight
  smallest; also applies to recipes with fewer than five paints


v0.07 (2026-10-25)
------------------
Fixed
- A single paint appeared as several shades on the sphere; it is now
  shown as one flat colour


v0.06 (2026-10-25)
------------------
Added
- Warhammer Dry range: 29 paints in 6 colour groups, selectable for
  recipes; Dry metallics (2) for reference only

Changed
- Reduced on-screen text: header hint, chart legend and info boxes removed;
  footer notes shortened to one line
- Swatch tooltip shows the layer a click will fill
- Paint types shown as "Base paint" / "Layer paint"
- Reference-only paints are dimmed
- "Paints needed" appears once the recipe contains a paint
- Examples and saved recipes combined in one "Recipes" section
- A single paint is shaded on the sphere
- Short hint below the sphere: drag to move the light
- Warhammer Shade grouped by colour
- Metallic and shade markers on swatches are now visible; metallics brighter
- Warhammer Colour swatches marked B (Base) or L (Layer) instead of a corner notch
- Warpfiend Grey listed as Layer paint

Removed
- Warhammer Colour: Ceramite White (discontinued)


v0.05 (2026-10-25)
------------------
Removed
- Edge highlight layer; recipes now have five layers


v0.04 (2026-10-24)
------------------
Changed
- Position numbers are shown only for paint series (Tone Pro,
  Warpaints Fanatic); removed from Warhammer Shade


v0.03 (2026-10-24)
------------------
Changed
- Footer credit now reads "Vibecoded by Duempelhuber"
- Revised change log


v0.02 (2026-10-24)
------------------
Added
- Warhammer Colour range: 130 Base and Layer paints in 18 colour groups
- Base paint indicator on swatches
- Warhammer Colour metallics and Warhammer Shade (reference only, not
  selectable for recipes)
- Brand tabs with range selection
- Recipe counters per brand and range

Changed
- Long colour groups wrap to a second line
- Each brand restores the last selected range


v0.01 (2026-10-24)
------------------
Initial release.

Added
- Warhammer Tone Pro: 30 colours, 5 tones each
- The Army Painter Warpaints Fanatic: 27 colour ranges, 170 paints
- Six-layer recipe: Deep shadow, Shadow, Base, Highlight, Top highlight,
  Edge highlight
- Combination of paints from different ranges in one recipe
- Sphere preview with smooth and stepped blending
- Adjustable light direction (mouse drag, arrow keys)
- Paints needed list
- Copy recipe to clipboard
- Example recipes; saving of custom recipes
- Offline operation as a single HTML file

Fixed
- Warpaints Fanatic, Black & Greys: position of Ironclad Grey
