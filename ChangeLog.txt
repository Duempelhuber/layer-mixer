Layer Mixer – Change Log
========================

Unreleased
----------
Added
- Oil paints are marked: "Oil" on the swatch, in the layer list and on the
  scheme cards (AK Abteilung 502, Scale75 Floww, AMMO Oilbrusher)
- AMMO Oilbrusher is its own range (was part of Weathering); schemes and
  saved files with the old paints keep working
- A bin on each part card in Schemes deletes the part (with Undo)
- New scheme asks for a name first
- Start is a tab at the top (Start, Mixer, Schemes, Paints) instead of an
  entry in the brand list
- Settings menu (gear, top right): Save to file, Save as, Load from file
  and Clear all
- Clear all removes everything Layer Mixer keeps in the browser and starts
  like the first time, with the example schemes, or empty if you choose;
  a warning lists what goes and has to be confirmed
- A note where the tool can't run (file previews, e.g. on iPhone):
  "Layer Mixer only works on desktop computers.", plus a line that a
  phone app is planned
Changed
- Preview: Contrast and Speedpaint tint everything under them instead of
  adding a dark band; over white they show their own colour
- Preview: band widths follow the role; deep shadow only in the deepest
  recesses, edge only on the edges, a drybrush covers most of the layer
  before it
- Layer roles are left-aligned
- Help reorganised and brought up to date (part menu, Undo, similar paints,
  preview, Missing and Copy missing paints, keyboard)
- Start page uses the full width; How it works is one larger panel; scheme
  cards stand out more, Example next to the name
- Progress bars for My paints are gold
- Marks on swatches are white; only on (nearly) pure white they are dark
- Clear is no longer in Paints, only in the settings menu

v0.41 (2026-11-08) – New look
-----------------------------
Changed
- New look: Schibsted Grotesk for text, Red Hat Mono for numbers,
  gold accent in the dark theme, neutral grey light theme, smaller
  corner radii, clearer type sizes
- Part tabs show the part's paints as a small strip
- Layers are numbered in the list; the layer marks in the chart are gold
  so they don't mix up with the tone numbers
- The part menu button is labelled "Part"
- "+N more" in the search shows the rest of the results
- Start page and Paints needed are plainer
- "nearly identical" instead of "same colour" for very close paints
- Removing a layer no longer shifts the list (windows 760 px and taller)

v0.40 (2026-11-07) – Scheme creator
-----------------------------------
Added
- Schemes: a scheme holds the parts of a model (armour, cloak, metal …),
  each part has 1 to 8 layers
- Every layer has a role: Primer, Deep shadow, Shadow, Base, Shade, Layer,
  Highlight, Edge, Drybrush or Glaze
- Add, remove (with Undo) and reorder layers by drag or Alt + arrow keys
- Part tabs in the Mixer; parts can be renamed, duplicated, moved,
  copied, cleared and deleted
- Schemes module: one card per part with its colours, paints and note,
  plus a note for the whole scheme
- Paints needed for the whole scheme, with a Missing filter and
  Copy missing paints
- Light theme; the theme follows the system, or is set to Light or Dark
- Examples: Imperial Fists and Death Guard
- Shift + Enter in the search puts the paint into the active layer
- Alt + 1, 2, 3 switch between Mixer, Schemes and Paints
- Layers by keyboard: up and down pick a layer, left and right reach
  its role, ≈ and ×; Tab moves past the whole list in one step

Changed
- Three modules in the header: Mixer, Schemes, Paints (was Mix and
  My paints)
- Washes, contrast, metallics, inks and technical paints can be used in
  layers; only paints without a colour value are refused
- The preview leaves out primer, a shade or wash darkens the layer
  before it, a glaze tints the layers after it
- Chart badges show the layer number in the open part
- Range tags always name the brand ("Warhammer · Shade")
- Everything is saved as you go; the note belongs to the part
- The save file also holds the schemes (format 2); older files still
  load
- Saved recipes become schemes with one part each; nothing is lost
- On low screens the preview stays in place and only the layer list
  scrolls

Removed
- Recipe chips, the example recipes and the Save / Update row
- The fixed five layers (Deep shadow … Top highlight)
- "Reference only"

v0.30 (2026-11-07)
------------------
Added
- Search: find any paint by name, range or brand from the field in the
  header (or press /); a result opens its range and marks the paint
- Similar paints: ≈ next to a recipe layer lists the closest paints of
  other ranges and brands, also only from your own paints; hovering one
  shows it on the shape, a click swaps the layer

Fixed
- The tooltip no longer shows a name twice (e.g. Vallejo White)


v0.20 (2026-10-28)
------------------
Added
- AMMO: IONIC Smart Colors, ATOM and AMMO Acrylic (with primers);
  Real Heavy Metal, IONIC Smart Drybrush, IONIC Smart Inks, Weathering
  and auxiliary products for reference
- Reaper: Master Series Core Colors as triads from shadow to highlight,
  Bones and Pathfinder Colors
- Green Stuff World: Acrylic Color, Opaque Colors, Airbrush Paint and
  Primer (bottles and spray cans); Dipping Ink, Inks, Metallic &
  Chameleon, Fluor, Effects & Dry Brush, Weathering & Textures, Dipping
  Ink Markers and auxiliary products for reference
- Favourite brands: star a brand to keep it at the top of the list, drag
  stars (or Alt + arrow keys) to change their order
- The recipe you are working on, its shape and blending are kept when
  the page is reloaded
- Works in narrow windows and on phones: the page becomes one column
- Keyboard section in the help; skip link to the recipe

Changed
- Brands are listed from A to Z ("The Army Painter" under A); the
  favourites star replaces the pin
- My paints: Save and Load sit at the top of the panel
- Paints needed lines up long range names
- Reference paints look the same in the legend as in the chart

Fixed
- Paints and recipes from a newer or older version, or from a renamed
  paint, are no longer dropped when you save
- Two open tabs no longer overwrite each other; deleting a recipe always
  deletes the one you clicked
- Loading a damaged or very large file can no longer freeze the page,
  and Load says so when the browser storage is full
- A broken address (e.g. #% at the end) no longer leaves the page empty;
  changing the range in the address works without reloading
- Clicking paints is much faster on Block and Cloth, and the arrow keys
  move the light smoothly
- Five tones and triads stay on one line at 1024 pixels
- Marking paints by dragging works with a finger
- Screen readers announce picked paints, missing paints and the recipe
  layer of a paint; focus no longer jumps to the top after changing a
  layer or deleting a recipe
- AMMO ATOM Matt White was shown black; Scale75 Inktense Cyan, AK Pastel
  Blue and the GSW Dipping Ink Markers had wrong colours; AK media
  and varnishes could be put into a recipe
- The tool only runs its own script (Content Security Policy)
