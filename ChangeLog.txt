Layer Mixer – ChangeLog
=======================

Version numbers: 0.01, 0.02, … Every published change gets a new version
and an entry at the top of this file.


v0.01 – 2026-10-24
------------------
First release.

Paint ranges
- Warhammer Tone Pro: 30 colours, 5 tones each, with colour type
- The Army Painter Warpaints Fanatic: 27 colour ranges, 170 paints

Features
- Recipe with six layers: Deep shadow, Shadow, Base, Highlight,
  Top highlight, Edge highlight
- Paints from different ranges can be combined in one recipe
- Shaded sphere preview, smooth or stepped
- Light direction adjustable by dragging on the sphere or with the
  arrow keys
- "Paints needed" list per range, including Tone Pro tone numbers
- Copy recipe as text
- Three example recipes; own recipes can be saved in the browser
- Runs completely offline as a single HTML file

Data
- Fanatic "Black & Greys": Ironclad Grey placed between Matt Black and
  Deep Grey
