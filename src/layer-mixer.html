    How it works

    Mixer

    • Pick a brand and a range on the left. Click a paint to put it into the active layer.
    • Each part has up to eight layers. Set the role with the button in front of the paint.
    • Add layer inserts below the active one. Drag the grip or press Alt + arrow keys to move a layer.
    • ≈ next to a layer shows similar paints from other ranges.
    • Star a brand to keep it on top, drag stars to reorder.

    Preview

    • Drag on the shape to move the light. Double-click resets it.
    • Change the shape, switch Smooth / Stepped, set the light strength with .
    • Primer is not drawn. A shade or wash darkens the layer before it.

    Schemes

    • A scheme holds all parts of a model, each with its layers.
    • Open in Mixer lets you change the paints of a part. Back in the browser returns to the scheme.
    • Paints needed lists every paint of the scheme and marks the ones you don't own.
    • Copy puts the scheme on the clipboard as text.
    • Click the name of a scheme or a part to rename it (in the Mixer: double-click a part).
    • Everything is saved in this browser as you go. Save to file in the scheme menu, or Save in Paints, keeps a copy as a file.

    Paints

    • Mark the paints you own: click, drag across, or click a row name.
    • Mine shows only your paints.
    • Save writes your paints and schemes to a file, Load reads it back. Save as picks a new file.

    Keyboard

    • / jumps to the search. Arrow keys and Enter open a paint in its range. Shift + Enter in the search puts the paint into the active layer.
    • Alt + 1, 2, 3 switch between Mixer, Schemes and Paints.
    • A adds a layer, Delete removes the active one, R changes its role.
    • In the layers, up and down pick a layer, left and right reach its role, ≈ and ×.
    • Arrow keys move through the brand list, F stars a brand, Alt + arrows reorders stars.
    • Arrow keys on the shape move the light. Enter on a row name marks the whole row in Paints.
