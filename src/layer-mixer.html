Layer Mixer

How it works

Mix

  1. Pick a brand and a range on the left. Pin a brand to keep it open.
  2. Click paints from dark to light. They fill the recipe from Deep shadow to Top highlight.
  3. Click a layer in the recipe to change it.

Preview

  • Drag on the shape to move the light. Double-click resets it.
  • Change the shape, switch Smooth / Stepped, set the light strength with .

Recipes

  • Name a recipe and save it, or start from an example.
  • Copy recipe puts it on the clipboard as text.
  • Paints needed lists what the recipe uses.

My paints

  • Mark the paints you own: click, drag across, or click a row name.
  • Mine shows only your paints (appears once you mark some). Paints you lack are highlighted in Paints needed.
  • Save writes your paints and recipes to a file, Load reads it back. Save as picks a new file.
